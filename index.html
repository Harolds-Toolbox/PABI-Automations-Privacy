GOOGLE EMAIL CONNECTION

Privacy notice

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This notice covers the Google email connection and development-request acknowledgement features in the PABI Automations development hub, operated by Harry Baker. For questions or requests about your information, contact harry@pabiautomations.co.uk.

What the connection does

An authorised operator can connect a Google account as the responder email for an organisation. While the local PABI app is running and connected, it uses that account to send acknowledgements to people who submit development requests through the organisation’s form or connected systems.

Information used

  • Google account email address and verified-email information, to identify the sender.
  • Google OAuth credentials and refresh tokens, to maintain the permission you grant.
  • Request information: requester name and email, department, system, submission date and description, to prepare the acknowledgement.
  • Delivery receipts and operational status, including request identifiers, sending state, time and the message identifier returned by Google, to track delivery and prevent duplicates.

Google permissions

The app requests basic Google sign-in/email identity and the Gmail permission to send email on your behalf (gmail.send). This feature does not request access to read your inbox, contacts, calendar or existing email messages. The connected account appears as the sender and reply address. Replies arrive in that account; this feature does not read them.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to identify the configured sender, send the requested acknowledgement, support the connection and avoid repeated messages. The acknowledgement contains the submitted request details and is addressed to the requester. Google processes the message and may keep a copy in the sender’s Sent folder under its own service settings. Existing organisation systems and Cloudflare store and synchronise request records and delivery coordination receipts. PABI sends the acknowledgement from the local hub; it does not upload Google sender credentials to those Cloudflare services.

The Google data used by this feature is not sold, used for advertising or credit decisions, or provided to train general-purpose AI models. Authorised operators may access request information for the operational purpose of handling the request. Service providers process information only as needed to deliver the relevant service; disclosures may also be required by law.

Storage and protection

Sender configuration is stored privately on the computer where the PABI hub runs and is excluded from its GitHub repository. Refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with the encryption key stored in the same protected local application area. File permissions limit access to the local account. Request records and delivery receipts are retained locally and, where applicable, in the organisation’s existing cloud systems. Email copies remain subject to the Google account’s retention settings. These protections do not prevent access by someone who controls the computer or the authorised account.

Retention and disconnection

OAuth credentials are retained until the account is disconnected, replaced or revoked. Choosing Disconnect in PABI removes the sender refresh token on that PC and stops sending through that connection. It does not automatically delete queued requests, delivery receipts, previously sent emails or connections on another PC. Requests and delivery receipts remain until removed through the relevant system or by an authorised operator; receipts help prevent duplicate acknowledgements.

You can revoke PABI’s access in your Google Account connections. Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion of information held for this feature. Some records may need to be retained for ongoing service, security or applicable obligations. For organisation-owned request information, your organisation may also need to handle your request.

Google API Limited Use

PABI Automations’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

Changes to this feature or its handling of information will be reflected in this notice and its update date.
